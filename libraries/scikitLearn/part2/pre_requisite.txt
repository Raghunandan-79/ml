    Mean And Centroid: 
        1. Mean: It will give us an average of the data.
        2. Centroid: It is the central point (average) of a cluster, acting as its
                     representative prototype, calculated as the mean of all data 
                     points within that cluster.

    Linear Algebra in Machine Learning
    ----------------------------------

    Linear Algebra is the branch of mathematics that deals with vectors, matrices, and linear transformations. 
    It forms the foundation of many machine learning algorithms and data representations.

    Key Concepts:
    --------------
        1. Scalars – Single numerical values.
        2. Vectors – Ordered lists of numbers representing data points or 
                     model parameters.
        3. Matrices – 2D arrays of numbers used for storing datasets and weights.
        4. Tensors – Generalization of matrices to higher dimensions (used 
                     in deep learning).
        5. Dot Product – Measures similarity between two vectors.
        6. Matrix Multiplication – Core operation for transformations and 
                                   neural network layers.
        7. Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors – Used in dimensionality reduction (PCA).
        8. Determinants and Inverses – Important in solving linear equations and 
                                       optimization.

    Applications in Machine Learning:
    ---------------------------------
        - Representing datasets as matrices (rows = samples, columns = features)
        - Performing transformations and projections (e.g., PCA)
        - Calculating gradients in optimization algorithms
        - Defining weights and activations in neural networks
        - Expressing loss functions and regularization mathematically

    Essential Formulas:
    -------------------
        1. Vector Dot Product: a · b = Σ (ai * bi)

        2. Matrix Multiplication: C = A × B

        3. Transpose: (Aᵀ)ij = Aji

        4. Determinant (2x2): |A| = ad - bc

        5. Eigenvalue Equation: A·v = λ·v

    Conclusion:
    -----------
        Linear algebra provides the language and tools that make modern machine 
        learning possible. From simple regression to deep neural networks, 
        understanding linear algebra helps you grasp how data is represented, 
        transformed, and optimized.
